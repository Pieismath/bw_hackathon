Citation Verification

Every supporting passage is checked against the source PDF using deterministic fuzzy matching, then independently judged for whether the citation actually supports the claim.

Adversarial Critique

A senior reviewer reads the paper, the extracted methodology, and the verification report, then issues three criticisms: missed, oversimplified, and alternative interpretation.

Replication Result

Replication engine output: Newey-West-adjusted returns, overlapping tranche construction, with survivorship and data-integrity disclosures.

Stress-Test Battery

Six families of perturbations rerun the canonical engine under altered conditions; the senior reviewer interprets the scorecard into a single implementability verdict.

Divergence Diagnosis

When replication disagrees with the paper's reported result, the diagnostician proposes single-parameter mutations, reruns the engine, and attributes the residual gap to a concrete cause.
