Live

Replication spec

Structured Pydantic contract produced by A1 (methodology extractor) and audited by A2. Every non-default is tied to a verbatim quote.

Quote verification

A2 walks every supporting quote: deterministic fuzzy-match against the PDF, then Haiku judgment on whether the quote actually supports the field's claim.

Adversarial critique (A3)

Opus reads the paper, A1 spec, and A2 report, then emits three criticisms: missed, oversimplified, alternative interpretation.

Backtest result

Engine output: Newey-West adjusted returns, overlapping tranches, survivorship-flagged provenance.

Provenance lineage

Every number in the pipeline traces back through ProvenanceRecord links to a data source or synthesizing agent.
